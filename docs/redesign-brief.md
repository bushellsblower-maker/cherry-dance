## Design notes

**Palette sampled from the live site**

- Black `#000000` and white `#FFFFFF` — dominant.
- Brand red `rgb(205, 9, 0)` / `#CD0900`, also at 65% opacity. Headings and hero subcopy.
- Dusty gold `rgb(176, 169, 134)` / `#B0A986`.
- Pale pink class tiles `rgba(239, 180, 195, 0.34)`.
- TeamUp / “CLICK HERE” teal `rgb(7, 65, 81)` / `#074151` (widget chrome, not brand).
- Default link blue appears on some Wix links.
- Motif: red cherries + white/red paint splatter on black. Logo wordmark is black on the splash.

**Tone**

Friendly, community, slightly homemade. “professional, supportive and fun”. First-person student quotes. Informal typos on the site (“beaut”, “wimping”, “Thankyou”, “perfom”, missing spaces in the address). Adult cabaret/hen content sits next to kids aerial and family dance without a visual split. Brand reads cherry / cabaret / paint-splatter, not a clean contemporary studio.

**UX issues a redesign should assume**

- Wix template: tiny nav, huge empty full-page scroll, copyright stuck at 2022.
- “Live Bookings” is copy, not a control. The real button is “CLICK HERE” and its href still has 2025 dates (client rewrites them).
- Embedded booking widget shows **America/Los Angeles**.
- Shop names and prices on Wix do not match the Clothing Kings catalog that the SHOP button actually opens.
- Contact address is missing a space. Cabaret email display and mailto disagree.
- Cabaret is a second brand (own site, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, ticket shop) barely connected from cherry-dance.com.
- No event calendar on the studio site. Newsletter promises events and lists none.
- Wedding page is thin (slideshow + one paragraph, no price, no CTA).
- Social feed module on the homepage appears empty.
- Accessibility: Grove ticket notes say stairs and no wheelchair access — relevant if the new site advertises those shows.
